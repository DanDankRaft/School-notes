Chromium as a tool of logistical power: A material political economy of open-source by Bolun Zhang and Davide Carpano, Big Data and Society Journal

- A key insight: Chrome was in part envisioned by an effort by google to make Web Apps happen! They thought W3C standards at the time did not enable it, so they wanted force through new standards that would enable web apps by building their own browser!

- One advantage of becoming the standard setter: You can change the standards so fast that your competitors cannot keep up - especially big problem for FOSS!!
