# Willa: War on Drug
- Public health angle for abolitionist arguments:
	- Lacks an egalitarian basis and fails to address structural issues and individual agency.
- Utilitarian failure ("the war on drugs failed!")


Moral rights argument:
- Individuals have a moral right to use drugs - bodily autonomy!
- The only moral justification for infringing on one's bodily autonomy is: if there is an alternative liberty in danger here, and we deem that alternative to be more important.

There is an incredibly strong moral rights argument in favor of 


Drug deprofitization: make a highly regulated, not-entirely-free, but legal market for drugs. The point is to make it *unprofitable* to sell drugs!

Harm Reduction based medical care: abstinence is not the solution for everyone, just make sure to reduce negative implication for people who do use drugs

Strong connection to capabilities approach and restorative justice.

If this approaches saves us a ton of money, what do we do with it?
- Involve a more participatory process with more parties.
- Reinvest in harmed communities.
- Create treatment programs that allow patients to stay with their local community.

- This model exists outside of doctor-patient care, because a lot of the harm reduction solutions don't require a lot of doctor involvement!!
