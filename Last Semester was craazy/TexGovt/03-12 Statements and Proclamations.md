# Types of statements
- Messages - messages from the governor to the legislature.
- General proclamations - these are still official documents. ex: disaster proclamations
- Legislative proclamations - session emergency items, veto statements, etc.
- Executive Orders
- Letters - communications to federal or state officials requesting action.

# Disaster Proclamations in TX
- Current disaster proclamations in TX:
	- The border
	- Drought
	- Flooding

- What governor can do once he declares a disaster through the Texas Disaster Act:
	- May issue executive orders and binding proclamations
	- May suspend regulatory statutes and state agency rues
	- May use resources of state and local governments, commander private property to cope with a disaster
	- May evacuate populations
	- Transfer disaster-related funds from other appropriation items.

- The governor needs to renew a disaster proclamation every 30 days, there's no limit on how often he needs to renew it.
- But the legislature can terminate a disaster at any time (provided that they're in session)
	- Problems with this:
		- The governor can suspend the law while a disaster proclamation is ongoing
		- The governor can veto a bill terminating a disaster
		- The governor can just.. not call a special session, forcing the legislature to wait until the next regular session.
> [!info] The governor is incentivized to extend the disaster as many times as necessary
> My reading: as many times as possible? Why would he revoke it.

- Why does the legislature want the governor to be in charge of disaster response?
	- They only meet once every 2 years
	- **Accountability** - if things go wrong, they want the governor to take the blame!
