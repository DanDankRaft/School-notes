# Legislative Budget Board
Composition:
- Lt. governor
- speaker of the house
- chairman of senate finance committee
- chairman of house appropriations committee
- chairman of house ways and means
- three members of the senate appointed by lt. governor
- three members of the house appointed by the speaker.
- uhhh something tlese I missed

- LBB is statutorily charged with preparing the first draft of the appropriations bill
- In practice, the LBB usually submits its budget in December before the regular session starts.
- Tex. Const. Art. 8 § 6 - "no money shall be drawn from the Treasury but in pursuance of specific appropriations made by law, nor shall any appropriation be made for a longer term than two years."
	- You can't spend a dime without it being appropriated!!
- The appropriations bill is also the only bill that has to pass every regular session.
- The state fiscal year is 9/1-8/31
### Sidenote: Federal budget process
- Budget resolution is divided into "functions," which is made up of 13 different bills.
- Unlike that, TX passes one appropriations bill.

### Methods of Finance
- General Revenue Funds
- General Revenue-Dedicated Funds
- Federal Funds
- Other Funds


- In the appropriation bill, there are "riders" that restrict how funding is allowed to be spent


- Every bill has to get a fiscal note saying how much money its gonna cost. The LBB determines this. It's much harder to pass bills with a non-zero fiscal note.

## Limitations on appropriations
- Constitutional limit - the growth rate of spending from non-dedicated tax revenue may not exceed the official estimate of the growth rate of the state's economy set by the LBB (statute uses "growth in personal income" as a proxy)
- Before each legislative session, the LBB adopts a projected state personal income growht rate from the current fiscal biennium to the next fiscal biennium

# Budget mechanisms outside the regular appropriations process
- Emergency appropriations
	- Governor is authorized to determine that an emergency exists requiring an appropriation of funds
