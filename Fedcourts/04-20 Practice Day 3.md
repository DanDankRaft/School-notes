# *Strickland v. Washington* ineffective assistance of counsel test
1. Counsel's performance "fell below an objective standard of reasonableness"
	- Strategic decisions (decisions about micro-scale things that usually don't involve decisionmaking from the) are not covered here.
2. Counsel's "deficient performance prejudiced the defense"
	- Actually made a difference in the outcome, in a way that hurt the client.


# B. 1983 claim
Why did we have to wait for a pardon? Because you cannot bring a 1983 claim for malicious prosecution unless the conviction was already invalidated or overturned (*Heck v. Humphrey*)
