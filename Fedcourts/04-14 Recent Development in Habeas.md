# Edwards v. Vannoy
593 US __ (2021)
## Background
 - Petitioner was convicted by 11/12 jury members.
 - He filed a federal habeas petition in 2015, arguing that the constitution required a unanimous jury conviction.
 - In 2020, while his writ for certoriari was pending in SCOTUS, the court decided *Ramos v. Louisiana*, which held that the constitution required a unanimous jury conviction.
