# Review of Last Class
- Localities are only liable if their "policy or custom" violates federal law (*Monell*)
- What counts as a valid "policy or custom"?
	- Local statute/ordinance.
	- Local agency regulation.
	- Decision by high-level official with policymaking power (*Pembaur*)
		- State law can determine who has policymaking power (*Pembaur*), but in the absence of a state statute, both parties can argue about this.
		- One incident *is* enough (*Pembaur*)
	- Failure to train:
		- Only if it demonstrates "deliberate indifference" to the risk that this kind of violation would occur.
		- One incident is typically *not* enough (*Tuttle*, *Connick*)
		- Logic - we want to only hold localities liable if it's actually their fault - not a bad employee. *§1983 doctrine towards localities wants to avoid respondeat superior.*
	- Local custom (typically only used in conjunction with failure to train)

- In practice, proving categories 3-5 is quite difficult.
