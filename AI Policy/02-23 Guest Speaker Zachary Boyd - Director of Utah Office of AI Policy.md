## What are obstacles in running a successful sandbox?
- Is there an actual legal barrier to what they're doing?
	- A lot of companies just want a gold star saying they're doing something legal
- Large, international and inter-state companies tend to not find the benefits particularly attractive.
- Sometimes, companies lack the resources to provide what they want to provide or to comply with regulations.
- Jurisdictional co-regulation - they might need permission from federal govt, local govt, insurance providers, etc..
- A lot of regulators want to make an experiment so narrow that it won't provide any value to consumers. It's a common point of pushback from regulators.

## Common concerns from companies
- What is the relationship with federal co-regulators? multi-jurisdiction stuff is hard!
- Do you want my IP? Do you want my personal data?
- What are the reporting requirements? How does FOIA interface with this?
- Can we do this at multiple states at once? This is an ongoing project, Utah is in early talks with other states to make that possible
- Can you relieve us from private liability?
