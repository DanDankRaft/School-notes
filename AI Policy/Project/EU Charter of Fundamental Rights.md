- Article 8 is all about the right to protection of personal data! The GDPR can be thought of as an implementation of this!
- There is no explicit "right to privacy" in the charter, but:
	- Article 7 - Respect for private and family life: "Everyone has the right to respect for his or her private and family life, home and communications."
	- Article 10 - Freedom of thought, conscience and religion: "this right includes freedom ... either alone or in community with others and in public or in private, to manifest religion or belief, in worship, teaching, practice and observance."
